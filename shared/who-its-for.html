Who it's for

Firms of about five to thirty people.

It suits a business where the principals are still close enough to the work to know where the week's going, and where somebody's already wondering whether AI could do some of it.

It's probably not for you if you're a sole trader, if you've more than about fifty staff and a transformation team already, or if you want someone to pick an AI tool for you before anyone's looked at the work.
