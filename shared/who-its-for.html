Who it's for

Firms of about five to thirty people.

A good fit

A business where the principals are still close enough to the work to know where the week's going, where the same jobs keep coming round, and where somebody's already wondering whether AI could take some of it off the team's plate.

Not a good fit

A business of one or two people, where the check will tell you straight away that a paid audit isn't worth it yet, and anyone who wants AI making the calls that their licence or their professional standards say a person has to make.
