How the audit works

A free call, then the audit.

First, 15 minutes on a call, where you tell me where the week goes and I tell you whether an audit's worth doing, and if it isn't, I'll say so.

Then the audit

  1. An hour with you, and 45 minutes each with up to three of the people who do the work.
  2. A written report on which jobs are worth changing, what each one's worth, and which ones AI should stay away from.
  3. A debrief where we go through it, ranked by effort and value, with a plan for the next few months.

After that, you can run the plan yourself, hand it to someone else, or bring me back to build it.
