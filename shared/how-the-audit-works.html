How the audit works

Two steps, and the first one's free.

The 15-minute call tells you whether an audit's worth doing at all. The audit tells you what to change, and what each change is worth on your own figures.

  1. An interview with you, about an hour, then 45 minutes each with up to three of the people who actually do the work, all of it recorded so that nothing gets lost.
  2. A written report on where the week goes, which workflows are worth changing and what each one's worth, worked out from your own hours and rates, and which ones AI should stay well away from.
  3. A debrief where I walk you through it, with everything ranked by effort and by what it's worth, and a plan for the next few months.
  4. After that it's your call whether you run the plan yourself, hand it to someone else, or bring me back to build it.
