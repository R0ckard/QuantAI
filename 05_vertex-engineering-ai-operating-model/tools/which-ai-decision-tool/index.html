QuantAI · Vertex Engineering AI operating model

Which AI for which task

Three approved platforms, one deliberate choice. Answer up to four questions and land on a defensible tool in about ten seconds.

Rule of thumb

Copilot for work inside your Microsoft files and inbox. Claude for careful drafting, structure and review of long documents. ChatGPT for open-ended thinking, research framing and quick generation.

Out of bounds for every tool: engineering design, calculation, verification or certification. A qualified engineer owns and signs the technical work, unaided by AI on the engineering content. This tool is for the words and admin around the work, never the engineering itself.
