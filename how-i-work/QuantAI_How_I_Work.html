How I handle your data

I'm selling governed AI, so I hold my own work to the same standard. Concretely:

Conversations are recorded only with your explicit consent.
Transcripts and documents live in an agreed, access-controlled location.
Client data stays in approved, business-tier tools with model-training turned off.
Identifiers are de-identified where practical during drafting.
Material output is reviewed by a qualified human before it's used.
Your data is retained only as long as needed and deleted on request.

The principle behind every build

AI drafts, a human decides.
Automation removes the repetitive effort; accountability stays with your people.
Captured once, reused by everyone.
Good prompts and standards become shared assets, so quality compounds instead of staying in one person's head.
You own it after handover.
The goal is capability that runs without me, not a dependency on me.
