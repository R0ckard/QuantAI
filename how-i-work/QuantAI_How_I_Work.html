Each one follows on from the audit, so nobody goes back over what your team has already told me.

1
We agree the scope
From the audit plan we pick what's being built, and write it down with the price before anything starts.
2

On the tools you already pay for, with a person checking everything before it's used.

We check the numbers
A month or so in, we compare the hours against the audit's figures, that way you can see what actually changed.
