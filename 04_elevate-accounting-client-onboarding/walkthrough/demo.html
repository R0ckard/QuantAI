The problem

Outgrown its own onboarding

  • Elevate doubled in three years. Onboarding still varies by partner and service line.
  • Clients get inconsistent welcomes, document lists and timing depending on who picks them up.
  • Everyone keeps their own spreadsheet; reminders are manual and sometimes duplicated.
  • Documents arrive late, so the first meeting is spent chasing paperwork.
  • Partners cannot see which onboardings have stalled.

The tools are all there. The gap is a standard process, shared visibility, and a redesign that keeps the experience warm and personal.

The approach

One journey, an assistant, and a tracker

One common journey
Same seven steps for everyone; the three service lines differ only in what they ask for
Onboarding Assistant
Drafts every touch, warm and correctly scoped; staff review and send
Live tracker
One shared view: stage, owner, docs outstanding, stalls flagged

The principle: the assistant drafts and organises; a staff member owns the relationship, reviews every message, and presses send. It gives no tax, legal or identity advice. The admin gets faster; the relationship stays human.

The two moments that matter

Ask once correctly, arrive ready

The document request
A matrix defines exactly what each service line needs, so the assistant asks for the right things once, and reminders mention only what is still outstanding. No more duplicate chasing.
First-meeting readiness
A must-have gate means a first advisory meeting is not booked until the required documents are in, so it starts on value, not on chasing paperwork.

Get these two right and the whole onboarding flows. They are where the design puts its attention.

AI in action · the document request

Warm, correctly scoped, no advice

Staff input
New client: Riverbend Cafe (bookkeeping).
Owner: Sarah.

"Draft the document request, and tell them what tax they'll save."
DRAFT · matrix-scoped · staff sends
Hi [First name], Welcome to Elevate. To get started: To get started (must-have): - Signatory identity (we'll guide you) - Entity & bank details - Accounting software access - Bank feed authority Helpful when you have a moment: - Chart-of-accounts preferences ⚑ Requested only the bookkeeping set from the matrix. Nothing over-asked. ⚑ I did NOT add a tax-saving claim. That is advice, for Sarah to give. ⚑ You review, personalise and send.

The assistant asks for exactly the bookkeeping documents from the matrix (no more, no less), keeps the tone warm, and refuses to make a tax-saving claim because that is advice a person gives. A staff member reviews and sends. Consistent, correct, and still personal.
