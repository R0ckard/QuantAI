# Onboarding Email Library

*Elevate Accounting. The firm's standard onboarding messages, one warm voice for everyone. The assistant drafts from these; a staff member personalises and sends.*

These are starting templates, not scripts. Square brackets are the personal bits a person fills in or the assistant drafts from real details. The tone is warm, clear, and professional: the way Elevate already talks to the people it likes.

## Tone notes (apply to all)

- Warm and human first, efficient second. A client should feel welcomed, not processed.
- Plain Australian English. Short sentences. No jargon, no dashes.
- Never promise a tax outcome, a saving or a timeline the owner has not confirmed.
- Every message names a real person as their contact.

---

## 1. Welcome

> Subject: Welcome to Elevate, [First name]
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Welcome to Elevate, we are really pleased to be working with [entity name]. I am [owner name], and I will be your main point of contact, so please reach out to me any time.
>
> To get you set up smoothly, here is what happens next: a quick note from us to confirm a few details, then a short list of documents to send through, and once those are in we will get you set up and book your first meeting. We will make it as easy as we can.
>
> [Owner's personal line here, for example acknowledging a referrer or a detail from the first conversation.]
>
> Looking forward to getting started.
>
> [Owner name]

## 2. Document request

> Subject: A few documents to get [entity name] started
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Thanks again for coming on board. To get things moving, here is what we will need.
>
> **To get started (these let us set you up):**
> [must-have items for the service line, from the matrix]
>
> **Helpful when you have a moment (no rush):**
> [nice-to-have items for the service line]
>
> You can send everything securely here: [secure upload link]. If anything is unclear or you would like a hand, just reply and I will help.
>
> [Owner name]

## 3. Reminder, day 3 (light)

> Subject: Just checking these reached you, [First name]
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Just a quick note in case my last email got buried. Whenever you have a moment, we are still waiting on:
> [outstanding must-have items only]
>
> No rush at all, and shout if anything is tricky to find.
>
> [Owner name]

## 4. Reminder, day 7 (offer to help)

> Subject: Anything we can do to make this easier?
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> I know gathering paperwork is nobody's favourite job. We are still missing:
> [outstanding must-have items only]
>
> If it is easier to jump on a quick call and go through it together, I am happy to. Just let me know a good time.
>
> [Owner name]

## 5. Handover

> Subject: You are all set, [First name], meet your team
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Great news, [entity name] is all set up. Going forward, [ongoing contact name] will be looking after your [service line] work, and you are in excellent hands. I have introduced you both here.
>
> Of course, I am always around if you need me. Thanks for a smooth start, it was a pleasure getting you set up.
>
> [Owner name]
