# Weekly Digest Template

*NorthStar Consulting. A short, per-team weekly summary drawn from the registers. The assistant drafts it; a manager glances and sends.*

Goes to each team lead every Monday. Built from the action, decision and risk registers, so it takes seconds to produce and never chases a person for an update.

---

## [Team] weekly digest, week of [date]

**Actions**

- **Due this week:** [n] ([list the highest-priority few, with owner and due date])
- **Overdue:** [n] ([list, with owner and how many days over])
- **Carried forward (stuck, 2+ times):** [n] ([list, these need attention])
- **Completed last week:** [n]

**Decisions recorded last week:** [n]
[one line each for the notable ones]

**Risks raised or escalated:** [n]
[one line each, with severity and owner]

**One thing to act on:** [the single most important item, usually the most-carried-forward overdue action]

---

*Drawn from the registers. A manager reviews before it goes out. The point is a 30-second read that surfaces what is stuck, not a report nobody opens.*
