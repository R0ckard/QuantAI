# Monthly Reporting Template

*NorthStar Consulting. A one-page management report from the registers, for the COO and the leadership team.*

Produced monthly from the action, decision and risk registers and the adoption measures. It is a management report, not a data dump: a few numbers that answer leadership's questions and one section on what needs a decision.
