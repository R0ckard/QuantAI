# Standard Minutes Template

*NorthStar Consulting. One record standard for every meeting. The assistant drafts to this; a reviewer approves before it is published.*

Fields in square brackets are filled from the transcript. The core five apply to every meeting; add the type-specific block for the meeting type.

### 5. Type-specific block

*Include the block for this meeting type:*

- **Client delivery / project:** client and engagement reference; client-facing vs internal split; items needing client sign-off.
- **Leadership / decision:** decision authority; decisions needing wider communication.
- **Internal team / status:** carried-forward actions from the last meeting (reviewed).
- **Sales / pursuit:** opportunity reference; next step and owner; commercial-sensitivity flag.
- **Governance / risk / PMO:** risk-register links; escalations; assurance or compliance items.

---

### Reviewer checklist (resolve before publishing)

- [ ] Every action has a confirmed owner and due date (no [OWNER TO CONFIRM] / [DUE DATE TO CONFIRM] left)
- [ ] Decisions and risks are accurate and complete
- [ ] Summary is usable with minor or no edits
- [ ] Confidentiality flags handled; client-facing vs internal split correct
- [ ] Carried-forward actions from the previous record reviewed

*Nothing is published until the reviewer has cleared this checklist. The assistant drafts; a human approves.*
