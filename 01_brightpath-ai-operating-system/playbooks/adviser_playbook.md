# Role Playbook, Financial Advisers

*What AI does and does not do in an adviser's day at BrightPath.*

## Your one rule

**AI drafts. You decide and you own it.** Every figure, recommendation and word that reaches a client is your professional responsibility, whether you typed it or an AI drafted it. Used this way, AI removes the blank page, not your judgement.

## Where AI helps you (and where it doesn't)

| Task | Use AI? | How |
|---|---|---|
| First draft of an SoA/RoA | ✔ | Advice-Prep Project → review against file + best-interests → sign |
| Rationale prose for a decided strategy | ✔ | `strategy-rationale` prompt; you supply the reasons |
| File notes from meetings | ✔ | `file-note` prompt; confirm actions/owners |
| Scope & needs summary | ✔ | `needs-summary` prompt; confirms understanding, not advice |
| **Deciding the strategy or products** | ✘ | Your judgement, not the model's |
| **Verifying figures/projections** | ✘ (AI never) | From the file/system; you verify |
| Client emails | ✔ | Client-Comms Project; you send |

## Your daily habits

1. **Placeholders first.** Draft with `[CLIENT]`, `[BALANCE]`, `[FUND]`; reinstate real details at review, inside firm systems.
2. **Approved tools only.** Claude Team / ChatGPT Team. Never a personal account for client work.
3. **Run the review checklist.** Every advice draft ends with it, use it; it's your evidence of best-interests review.
4. **If it's advice, you sign it.** No exceptions.
5. **Improve the library.** Found a better prompt? Give it to your AI Champion so everyone gets it.

## What good looks like

You spend less time rebuilding boilerplate and more time on strategy and clients. Your drafts are consistent, your file notes are complete, your reviews are faster because drafts arrive in a known shape, and nothing reaches a client that you didn't verify and own.

## When in doubt

Ask your **AI Champion** (first line) or the **Head of Advice** (advice standard). If you're unsure whether data should go into a tool, don't paste it; ask. The governance framework (`../docs/04_governance_acceptable_use.md`) is two pages; read it once.

---

### Client Services, quick version

Same one rule. Use the Client-Comms Project to triage and draft; **send every message yourself**; escalate anything that smells like advice or is sensitive to an adviser, the Project will draft the holding reply for you. Placeholders for specifics, approved tools only.
