# Prompt, Scope & needs summary

**Purpose.** Draft a concise scope-and-needs summary from discovery notes, to confirm understanding before advice is prepared.
**When to use.** After a discovery/fact-find meeting, before drafting advice.
**Inputs.** Discovery notes (de-identified).

---

## Prompt

```
From my discovery notes below, draft a short "here's what we understand"
summary for a BrightPath client, to confirm before we prepare advice. Use ONLY
my notes; flag anything unclear as [[CONFIRM: …]].

Discovery notes:
<paste>

Produce:
- What matters most to you (goals, in the client's own framing)
- Your current situation (factual, brief)
- What you've asked us to help with (scope)
- What's out of scope for now
- What we need from you to proceed

Warm, plain English. This confirms understanding, it is NOT advice.
```

## Guardrails
- No recommendations or advice; confirmation of scope only. Flags gaps rather than assuming.

## Quality checks
Firm-wide standard, plus: explicitly states scope and out-of-scope; contains no recommendation.
