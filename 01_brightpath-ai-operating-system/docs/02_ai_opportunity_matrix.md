# Prioritised AI Opportunity Matrix

*Figures are scenario estimates under the stated assumptions.*

Six priority workflows were assessed (per the brief) on **value** (frequency × time saved × quality/risk reduction) and **feasibility** (effort, data sensitivity, change required). Value and feasibility are scored 1-5; **Priority = Value × Feasibility**. This is a transparent, adjustable model, the weights are visible so process owners can challenge them.

## Scoring

| # | Workflow | Freq. | Time-saving potential | Value | Feasibility | Priority | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Advice preparation** (SoA/RoA first drafts, file notes) | High | High | 5 | 4 | **20** | **1, Pilot** |
| 2 | **Client communications** (email triage & drafting) | Very high | Medium | 4 | 5 | **20** | **1, Pilot** |
| 3 | Meeting capture & actions | High | Medium | 4 | 4 | 16 | 3 |
| 4 | Proposals & engagement letters | Medium | Medium | 3 | 4 | 12 | 4 |
| 5 | Internal knowledge & search | High | Medium | 4 | 3 | 12 | 4 |
| 6 | Marketing content | Medium | Medium | 3 | 3 | 9 | 6 |

## Why these two pilots

**Advice preparation** is the firm's most valuable knowledge work: high-frequency, high-time-cost, and the place where a firm-standard first draft removes the most duplicated effort, *provided* the human-review gate is watertight. It's the workflow that proves AI can touch regulated output safely.

**Client communications** is the highest-frequency workflow in the building and the easiest to standardise, so it delivers visible wins fast and builds staff confidence. High feasibility (low data sensitivity when done right, no new tools) makes it the ideal confidence-builder to run alongside the harder advice pilot.

Together they cover the two archetypes the other four workflows fall into, **high-value/regulated** and **high-volume/low-risk**, so the patterns proven here transfer to workflows 3-6.

## The other four (summarised, for the roadmap)

- **Meeting capture & actions (rank 3):** strong candidate for phase 2; pairs naturally with the QuantAI meeting-minutes pattern already prototyped elsewhere.
- **Proposals & engagement letters (rank 4):** a templated Claude Project fed by the firm's fee and service library; low risk, medium reward.
- **Internal knowledge & search (rank 4):** highest change-effort because it depends on consolidating SharePoint/Dropbox first; sequence after the knowledge cleanup.
- **Marketing content (rank 6):** real value but least urgent; a fast follow once brand voice is captured.

## How to read this

Priority is a **decision aid, not a verdict.** The scores are deliberately simple and visible so the Steering Group can re-weight them (e.g. if risk appetite changes, drop Advice's feasibility and Client Comms leads alone). The matrix is included as a live sheet in the benefits tracker so it can be re-run.
