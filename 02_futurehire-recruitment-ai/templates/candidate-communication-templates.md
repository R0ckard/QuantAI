# Candidate Communication, Template Library

*Firm-standard starting points for the most common candidate messages. A recruiter always personalises and sends. Pair with the candidate-comms prompts for a tailored draft.*

Each template keeps FutureHire's voice, warm, clear, respectful, and applies the fairness rule: nothing about protected attributes, no detailed critique in declines.

## 1. Application received
> Hi [CANDIDATE], thanks for applying for [ROLE], we've got your application and it's with me now. I'll be in touch by [DATE] with next steps. In the meantime, feel free to reply with any questions. [RECRUITER]

## 2. Interview invitation
> Hi [CANDIDATE], great news, we'd love to talk with you about [ROLE]. It'll be a [FORMAT] with [WHO], about [DURATION]. Are you free [OPTIONS]? Here's what to expect: [PREP]. Reply and we'll lock it in. [RECRUITER]
*(Or generate a tailored version with the `interview-invite` prompt.)*

## 3. Keeping-you-posted (holding)
> Hi [CANDIDATE], a quick update on [ROLE], [STATUS in plain terms]. Next step is [NEXT], and I expect to know more by [DATE]. Thanks for your patience; I'll keep you posted. [RECRUITER]

## 4. Respectful decline
> Hi [CANDIDATE], thank you for your time with [ROLE], I really appreciated our conversation. On this occasion we're progressing other candidates, but I'd genuinely like to keep in touch for future roles that fit. Wishing you all the best, and do stay in contact. [RECRUITER]
*(Keep declines brief and warm, no itemised reasons. Use the `respectful-decline` prompt for tailoring.)*

## 5. Offer / next steps
> Hi [CANDIDATE], exciting news on [ROLE], [NEXT STEP, e.g. we're preparing an offer / moving to references]. Here's what happens next: [DETAIL]. I'll be your point of contact throughout, any questions, just ask. [RECRUITER]

---
**How to use:** start from the template, or run the matching prompt for a tailored draft, then personalise for the individual. Never send un-personalised, and never send without reading it as yourself.
