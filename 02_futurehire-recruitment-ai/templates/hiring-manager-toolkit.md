# Hiring-Manager Toolkit

*A short, client-facing toolkit recruiters use to set expectations and run consistent, professional client relationships. Pairs with the Client-Updates Project.*

## 1. What a good update looks like

Every FutureHire hiring-manager update, per role, answers three questions in under a minute's read:

1. **Where are we?** Candidates by stage (as counts or non-identifying references).
2. **What's next?** The immediate next step and timing.
3. **What do we need from you?** Any decision or input required to keep momentum.

## 2. Update cadence (agree at kickoff with each client)

| Role urgency | Update cadence |
|---|---|
| Hot / priority | Twice weekly + on any material change |
| Standard | Weekly |
| Slow-burn / talent pipelining | Fortnightly |

Consistency beats frequency, pick a cadence and hold it. A reliable weekly update builds more trust than sporadic bursts.

## 3. Confidentiality standard

- Candidates are referred to by stage/count or non-identifying reference unless the candidate has **consented** to be named to the client.
- Sensitive candidate detail is never shared.
- This protects candidates and keeps FutureHire on the right side of privacy obligations.

## 4. Intake checklist (start every role right)

Capturing this up front makes every later ad, summary and update faster and better:

- Role, must-haves vs nice-to-haves (kept tight), team/context
- Package range and genuine selling points
- Location / work model
- Process & timeline, who interviews, decision-makers
- Update cadence and preferred channel

## 5. Handling a difficult conversation

When a role stalls or a strong candidate withdraws, use the `delay-holding-update` prompt: be honest, reassuring and specific about the next step, no blame, no over-promising. Clients remember how you handle the hard moments.
