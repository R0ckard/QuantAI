# Prompt, Delay / holding update

**Purpose.** Draft a professional holding update when a role is slow or blocked, keeping the client informed and confident.
**Inputs.** Role, reason for delay, what's being done, expected next step.

---

## Prompt

```
Draft a short, professional holding update for a hiring manager when a role is
moving slowly. Be honest and reassuring without over-promising. Use only what
I give you.

Role: [ROLE]   Reason / context: <...>   What we're doing: <...>
Expected next step & timing: <...>

Keep it brief, warm, and confidence-building. No blame, no guarantees. End with
a clear next check-in time.
```

## Guardrails
- Honest, no over-promising, no blame; candidate confidentiality preserved; recruiter sends.

## Quality & fairness checks
Firm-wide checks; sets a realistic next step; maintains client confidence honestly.
