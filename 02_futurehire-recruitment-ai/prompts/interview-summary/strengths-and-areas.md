# Prompt, Strengths & areas to explore

**Purpose.** Draft a concise, evidence-based "strengths / areas to explore" block for a shortlist or client update, without straying into a decision.
**Inputs.** The structured summary or notes; the role requirements.

---

## Prompt

```
From my notes/summary, draft a short "strengths and areas to explore" block
against the role requirements, for the recruiter to use in a shortlist.

Rules: evidence-based only; job-relevant; no protected attributes or proxies;
no "hire/don't hire", strengths and open questions only.

Role requirements: <list>
Notes/summary: <paste>

Produce:
- Strengths (evidence-based, tied to requirements)
- Areas to explore (open questions for the next stage)
```

## Guardrails
- No recommendation; evidence-based; fairness rules apply.

## Quality & fairness checks
Firm-wide checks; framed as strengths + open questions, never a verdict.
