# Prompt, Offer / next-steps (candidate)

**Purpose.** Draft a warm next-steps or offer-stage message. **Not** a formal contract, that's handled by the client/legal.
**Inputs.** Role, the next step (reference check, offer being prepared, etc.), timing.

---

## Prompt

```
Draft a warm next-steps message for a candidate at the offer/late stage
(recruiter reviews and sends). Use only my details.

Role: [ROLE]   Next step: <e.g. references, offer in preparation>   Timing: <...>

Convey genuine positivity, set clear expectations on what happens next and when,
and invite any questions. Do NOT state formal offer terms unless I've given them
exactly, use placeholders. Warm, clear, professional.
```

## Guardrails
- No invented offer terms; placeholders; sets clear expectations; recruiter sends.

## Quality & fairness checks
Firm-wide checks; terms only if supplied; expectations clear.
