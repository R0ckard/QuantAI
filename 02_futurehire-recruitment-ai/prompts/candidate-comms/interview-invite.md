# Prompt, Interview invitation (candidate)

**Purpose.** Draft a clear, welcoming interview invitation with all the practical details.
**Inputs.** Role, format, who they'll meet, timing options, what to prepare/bring.

---

## Prompt

```
Draft a warm, clear interview invitation for a candidate (recruiter reviews and
sends). Use only my details; placeholders for anything missing.

Role: [ROLE]   Format: <e.g. video / on-site>   Meeting: [WHO], [WHEN options]
Prep / what to expect: <...>

Include: a warm opener, the practical details (who/when/where/how long), what to
expect and how to prepare, and an easy way to confirm or reschedule. Friendly,
professional, respectful of their time.
```

## Guardrails
- No invented times/links; placeholders for specifics; accessible, respectful tone.

## Quality & fairness checks
Firm-wide checks; details correct on send; offers a reschedule path.
