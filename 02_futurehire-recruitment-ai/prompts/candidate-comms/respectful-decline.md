# Prompt, Respectful decline (candidate)

**Purpose.** Draft a warm, respectful "not progressing this time" message that treats the candidate well and protects the relationship.
**When to use.** Declining a candidate. **Not** for giving detailed assessment reasons that could be unfair or legally risky.
**Inputs.** Role, stage reached, whether to keep in touch.

---

## Prompt

```
Draft a warm, respectful message letting a candidate know they're not
progressing for [ROLE]. A recruiter reviews and sends it.

Reached stage: [STAGE]   Keep on file / future roles? <yes/no>

Requirements:
- Genuinely warm and human; thank them for their time.
- Brief and kind. Do NOT give detailed critique or itemised assessment
  reasons, a short, general, respectful message is both kinder and safer.
- No comments on protected attributes or anything not job-relevant.
- If keeping in touch, say so warmly and specifically.
- Leave them feeling respected by FutureHire.
```

## Guardrails
- No detailed/again-unfair critique; no protected-attribute content; warm and brief; recruiter sends.

## Quality & fairness checks
Firm-wide checks, plus: no risky itemised reasons; respectful tone; consistent with a fair process.
