# Use-Case Prioritisation Matrix

*Figures are scenario estimates under the stated assumptions.*

Use cases were scored on **value** (frequency × time saved × reuse), **candidate impact** (does it improve or risk the candidate experience, higher is better/safer), **feasibility** (effort, data sensitivity, change required) and **risk** (fairness/privacy exposure, lower is better). The brief asks for three prototypes; the scoring confirms which three.

Scores are 1-5. **Priority = Value × Feasibility × Candidate-impact ÷ Risk**, rounded, a transparent, adjustable model.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Interview summarisation** | 5 | 4 | 4 | 2 | **40** | **Pilot** |
| **Job-ad drafting** | 4 | 4 | 5 | 2 | **40** | **Pilot** |

| Bullhorn auto-population | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 4 | Phase 2 (config first) |
| Candidate screening / ranking |, |, |, | 5 | **Excluded** | Out of scope & out of bounds |

## Why these three pilots

**Hiring-manager updates** carry the lowest risk (no candidate-facing decision content) and highest feasibility, so they build trust fastest, the ideal confidence-builder given FutureHire's history of failed automation.

**Job-ad drafting** is high-frequency and high-feasibility, and it's where inconsistency costs the most editing time today. It also lets us demonstrate **inclusive-language** checking, turning a fairness risk into a fairness *feature*.

**Interview summarisation** is the highest-value knowledge work: it standardises the candidate story, makes shortlists reusable, and captures the judgement that currently walks out the door, provided summaries stay strictly evidence-based and job-relevant.

Together the three cover the brief's success metrics (≥40% less admin time; 50% faster candidate-response prep; ≥90% quality/tone) and the two archetypes the other use cases fall into.

## The explicit exclusion

**Candidate screening, ranking or selection is excluded, deliberately, not for scope reasons alone.** Automated screening is where recruitment AI does the most harm (bias, opacity, unfair outcomes) and it is an explicit exclusion in the brief. The AI in this solution assists a recruiter with drafting and documentation; it never decides who progresses. This line is a selling point, not a limitation, it's what makes the solution safe to adopt.

## How to read this

Priority is a decision aid; the weights are visible so the Steering Group can re-weight for their own risk appetite. The matrix is included as a live sheet in the benefits tracker so it can be re-run.
