# 90-Day Rollout Roadmap & Benefits Realisation

*Indicative plan aligned to the brief's 10-week engagement, then a 90-day adoption horizon. Designed around FutureHire's key risk: rebuilding trust in automation.*

## Engagement (weeks 1-10), from the brief

| Phase | Weeks | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Mobilise & discover | 1-2 | Kickoff, access, recruiter/leader interviews, baseline measures, pilot group named |
| Analyse & design | 2-4 | Current-state findings, prioritisation, future-state design, governance, candidate-journey principles |
| Prototype & test | 4-7 | Configure the three pilot Projects + prompt/template libraries; user testing with the six-person pilot group |
| Enable & hand over | 8-10 | Train-the-trainer, final docs, Bullhorn config recs, rollout plan, acceptance |

## 90-day adoption (post-handover), trust-led

Because past automation failed on adoption, the rollout is sequenced to **earn trust before scaling**.

**Days 0-30, Prove it with the pilot desk.**
The six pilot recruiters run all three workflows live. AI Champions hold weekly office hours. Capture baseline vs actual in the benefits tracker. Publish the first "what good looks like" examples.
*Gate:* pilot users report ≥40% less time on targeted tasks; ≥90% quality; zero fairness/privacy breaches.

**Days 31-60, Expand to willing desks.**
Roll to recruiters who opt in first (momentum beats mandate). Refine prompts/templates from real use. Configure the Bullhorn fields and note types. Candidate-comms templates go live.
*Gate:* adoption growing; recruiter-rated usefulness ≥4/5; candidate-response prep down ≥50%.

**Days 61-90, Standardise.**
Roll to remaining desks with the proven pattern. Firm-level benefits review with leadership; decide phase-2 (scheduling/follow-up tooling, Bullhorn auto-population).
*Gate:* leadership approves the standard, controls and ownership model.

## Benefits tracker (the living measure)

Built reproducibly by `tools/benefits_tracker.py` → `deliverables/FutureHire_Benefits_Tracker.xlsx`. It carries the editable assumptions, the three pilot models (with % reduction and hours/value reclaimed), the live prioritisation matrix, and an adoption log where scenario estimates are replaced by measured pilot results, turning the model into a real management dashboard.

## What success looks like at day 90

Three workflows demonstrably faster and more consistent, recruiters *choosing* to use them (the real test after a failed past attempt), a fair and private process the agency can show clients, and leadership holding measured evidence to decide how far to take AI next.
