# AI Governance, Fairness, Privacy & Human Control

*FutureHire Recruitment. Practitioner-level governance for demonstration; **not** legal advice. A real deployment would be reviewed by the firm's Privacy Lead and employment-law adviser.*

Recruitment AI touches people's livelihoods, so this framework leads with fairness and privacy, not just data hygiene. It's the one-page rule set every recruiter reads and signs.

## 1. The three rules that matter most

1. **AI never screens, ranks, shortlists or decides.** It assists a recruiter with drafting and documentation. A recruiter makes every assessment and every candidate decision, and owns every output before it goes to a candidate or client. The tool has no say in who progresses.
2. **Assessment language is evidence-based and job-relevant only.** Interview summaries and candidate write-ups describe what the candidate demonstrated against the role's requirements, never inferences about age, gender, ethnicity, accent, appearance, family status, health, or any protected attribute, and never proxies for them.
3. **Candidate data stays private and in approved tools.** Only business-tier tools (Claude Team, ChatGPT Team) with model-training off. De-identify where practical. Never paste candidate personal data into personal or free AI accounts.

## 2. Fairness controls (built into the workflows)

- **Inclusive job ads.** The job-ad workflow checks drafts for gendered, ageist or exclusionary language and for unnecessary "requirements" that deter diverse applicants, and flags them for the recruiter.
- **Evidence-only summaries.** The interview-summarisation prompt is constrained to job-relevant evidence and explicitly instructed to omit protected attributes and unfounded characterisations; it surfaces a fairness check for the recruiter.
- **No proxy discrimination.** Recruiters are trained not to ask the AI to infer, and the prompts refuse to infer, sensitive attributes or "culture fit" judgements that mask bias.
- **Human review of every candidate-facing artefact.** The recruiter is the fairness backstop; the tools make that review easy by flagging what to check.

## 3. Privacy & data handling

| Data type | Example | May use with AI? | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-personal / generic | Job-ad tone, generic templates | Yes | Approved tools |
| Candidate personal information | Name, contact, CV content, history | Restricted | De-identify where practical; approved Team tools only; never personal accounts |
| Sensitive information | Health, background that reveals protected attributes | Avoid | Do not paste into any AI tool; keep in Bullhorn |

Alignment: Australian **Privacy Act 1988** and the **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)** govern candidate personal information; **candidate consent and privacy notices remain the client's responsibility** (per the brief), and this framework operationalises careful handling on top of that.

## 4. The human-review gate (must not be skipped)

Nothing reaches a candidate or client without a recruiter reviewing it. Specifically:

- **Job ads:** recruiter reviews for accuracy, inclusive language and brand before posting.
- **Interview summaries:** recruiter confirms the summary reflects the interview and contains only job-relevant evidence before it's saved or shared.
- **Hiring-manager updates:** recruiter checks accuracy and confidentiality before sending.

The gate is built into the prompts (each ends with a review checklist) so it's the easy path.

## 5. Red lines, AI must not

- Screen, rank, shortlist, reject or select candidates, or recommend those decisions.
- Infer or record protected attributes, or "culture fit"/personality judgements that proxy for them.
- Process candidate personal data in personal or free AI accounts.
- Scrape or automate LinkedIn, or ingest data the agency isn't permitted to use.
- Be presented to candidates as a human, or send anything a recruiter hasn't reviewed.

## 6. Roles

- **Privacy Lead**, owns candidate-data handling and this framework's privacy controls.
- **Recruitment Director**, owns the fairness and service standards.
- **Operations Director**, owns tools, access and Bullhorn configuration.
- **AI Champions**, first-line "can I use AI for this?" support; audit a sample of outputs for fairness.
- **All recruiters**, read, sign, apply; when unsure, don't paste, ask.

## 7. Why this is a selling point

A recruitment agency that can show clients and candidates a **documented, human-controlled, bias-aware** approach to AI has a competitive advantage, not a compliance burden. This framework is designed to be shown, not hidden.
