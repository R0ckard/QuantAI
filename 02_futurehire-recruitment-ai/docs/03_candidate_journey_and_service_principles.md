# Candidate Journey & Service Principles

*How AI assistance shows up across the candidate experience, and the standards it must uphold.*

AI is being introduced into a **human** process. These principles keep the candidate experience at the centre, so efficiency never comes at the cost of respect, fairness or clarity.

## Service principles (the standards every AI-assisted touchpoint must meet)

1. **A person is always responsible.** Every candidate-facing message is reviewed and sent by a recruiter. Candidates deal with people, not bots.
2. **Fair and evidence-based.** Assessment language reflects job-relevant evidence only, never assumptions tied to a candidate's background, and never protected attributes.
3. **Timely and human.** Faster drafting means candidates hear back sooner and with warmth, the point of the time saved is *better* communication, not just less of it.
4. **Private by default.** Candidate data is handled with care, kept in approved systems, and never used to train models.
5. **Consistent, not robotic.** A firm-standard tone that still sounds like a person, templates are a starting point the recruiter makes their own.

## The journey, and where AI assists

| Stage | What the candidate experiences | Where AI assists (recruiter-controlled) |
|---|---|---|
| **Attraction** | Finds a clear, inclusive job ad | Job-ad drafting, consistent, inclusive-language-checked ads |
| **Application & screening** | A fair, human review of their application | *No AI decisioning.* Recruiter screens; AI is not used to rank or reject |
| **Interview** | A professional, well-prepared conversation | Interview summarisation, structured, evidence-based notes after the fact |
| **Feedback & updates** | Prompt, respectful updates, even a "no" handled well | Candidate-communication templates (recruiter reviews and sends) |
| **Offer & onboarding** | Clear, timely communication | Templates for offer/next-steps comms |
| **Client side (parallel)** |, | Hiring-manager updates, clear pipeline updates to the client |

## The line we will not cross

At the **application & screening** stage, where AI could most easily be misused to rank or reject candidates, the solution deliberately does nothing automated. Screening stays a human judgement. This is the single most important design decision in the whole engagement, and it protects candidates, the agency's reputation, and its legal position at once.

## What "good" looks like for candidates

Candidates get quicker, warmer, clearer communication and a demonstrably fair process, while recruiters spend the reclaimed time on the human parts of the job that actually win placements. Efficiency and candidate experience pull in the same direction, not against each other.
