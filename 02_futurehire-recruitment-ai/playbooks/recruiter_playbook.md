# Role Playbook, Recruiters

*What AI does and does not do in a FutureHire recruiter's day.*

## Your one rule

**AI drafts, you decide.** It gives you back time on typing and formatting so you spend more of your day with candidates and hiring managers. It never screens, ranks or decides, that judgement is yours, and it's what clients pay for.

## Where AI helps you (and where it doesn't)

| Task | Use AI? | How |
|---|---|---|
| Drafting a job ad | ✔ | Job-Ads Project → review inclusive-language flags → post |
| Writing up an interview | ✔ | Interview-Summaries Project → verify it reflects the interview → save |
| Hiring-manager updates | ✔ | Client-Updates Project → check accuracy & confidentiality → send |
| Candidate emails (invite/decline/offer) | ✔ | Candidate-comms templates → personalise → send |
| **Deciding who progresses** | ✘ | Your judgement, never the AI's |
| **Ranking / scoring candidates** | ✘ (AI never) | Not done, by design |
| Anything with protected attributes | ✘ | Never, keep assessment job-relevant |

## Your daily habits

1. **Placeholders and de-identify.** Draft with `[CANDIDATE]`, `[CLIENT]`, `[SALARY]`; keep candidate personal data out of prompts where you can.
2. **Approved tools only.** Claude Team / ChatGPT Team, never a personal account for candidate or client work.
3. **Run the check.** Every prompt ends with a checklist, use it. For summaries, the fairness check is the important one.
4. **You send everything.** No candidate or client ever receives an unreviewed AI message.
5. **Improve the library.** A better prompt or template? Give it to your AI Champion so the whole desk gets it.

## What good looks like

Less time typing ads, notes and updates; more time on relationships. Consistent, professional, fair output every time, and a process you can be proud to describe to a candidate or client.

## When in doubt

Ask your **AI Champion** (first line) or the **Recruitment Director** (service standards) / **Privacy Lead** (candidate data). If you're unsure whether to put something in a tool, don't; ask. The governance framework (`../docs/04_governance_fairness_privacy.md`) is two pages; read it once.
